<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Caller-supplied date range and timezone to `build_window` | The tests here are the enforcement mechanism for the guards on that boundary |
| Caller-supplied `user_id` to the aggregate's WHERE clause | The single-user schema makes a missing predicate invisible without a deliberate second-user test |
| Test fixtures to the shared session-scoped container | A committing or colliding fixture corrupts every later test in the session |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-04-02 | Information Disclosure | A missing `user_id` predicate in `weekly_set_rows` | mitigate | `test_rows_are_scoped_to_one_user` builds a second user's workout in the same window and asserts the two result sets share no `workout_id`, with a non-vacuous guard asserting the second user's fixture actually produced rows |
